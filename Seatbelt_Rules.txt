Seatbelt Rules

Key:
"Included with"= $0 charge, seatbelt is auto added with listed interiors.
"Adds D30 with", "+D30"= D30 $1,495 auto-added, plus seatbelt charge ($595)
"Not available with"= Cannot be selected.
"Not recommended with"= "Adds D30 with"

379 Orange Seat belt
1LT, 1LZ, 2LT, 2LZ: Adds D30 with (HUQ, HU7, HUL, HUR) Adrenaline Red interiors.
3LT, 3LZ: Not available with (HVZ) Asymmetrical Adrenaline Red/Jet Black interior or (HAG) Asymmetrical Santorini Blue/Jet Black interior.
Adds D30 with (HU2, HUA, HUU, HZP) Adrenaline Red interiors, (HNK) Adrenaline Red Dipped interior, (EJH, EPX) Very Dark Atmosphere interiors, (H8T) Santorini Blue interior.
Included with (HUW, HUX) Habanero interiors.

3N9 Natural Seat belt

2LT, 2LZ: Recommended with (H1Y, HTM, HTP) Jet Black interior or (HTN, HTQ, HUV) Natural interior. +D30 with all other interiors.
3LT, 3LZ: Recommended with (HMO, HTE, HTT, HU0, HVV, HXO) Jet Black interiors, (HUB, HUC) Ultimate Suede Jet Black interiors or (HTG, HUE) Natural interiors. Included with (HUF, HZN) Natural Dipped interiors and (EJH, EPX) Very Dark Atmosphere interiors.
Not available with (HVZ) Asymmetrical Adrenaline Red/Jet Black interior or (HAG) Asymmetrical Santorini Blue/Jet Black interior.
+D30 with all other interiors.
